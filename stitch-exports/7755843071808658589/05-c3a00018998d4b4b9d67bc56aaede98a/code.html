ABOUT SCALESIGHT

I started ScaleSight to make deeper planning analysis useful before a brand commits more cash.

Founder Profile

Arman Mehdi

Founder, ScaleSight | Data scientist

Operational Scope

Analytical demand models, supply pipeline evaluation, and direct inventory capital risk review.

I’m Arman, a data scientist. In consumer brands, a forecast is only one input to a much larger decision: what to buy or produce, how much, when, and what the choice means for cash and availability.

ScaleSight brings data-science analysis and supply-chain review together around the commitments a brand has to make. We work from agreed sales, inventory, incoming supply and lead-time data, investigate changing demand, and compare the options. We maintain the analysis and bring the team a clear recommendation; the team keeps the final call.

Larger brands often hire planners, analysts and data-science specialists around their systems. ScaleSight gives growing brands access to analytical support without building every capability internally. For lean teams, that adds planning capability. For established teams, it adds bandwidth around a defined investigation.
