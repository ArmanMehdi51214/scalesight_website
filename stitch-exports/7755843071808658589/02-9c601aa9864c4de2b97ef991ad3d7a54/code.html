From a demand signal to a decision your team can act on

ScaleSight works with the data and planning context you already have. We investigate what changed, compare the available options, and bring your team a reviewed recommendation. The workspace supports the service; your team does not have another planning tool to operate.

Talk through a planning decision

Start with the commitment, then agree the inputs.

01

Choose the decision

Agree the upcoming buy, production, replenishment or allocation decision, the products and channels involved, the timing and the decision owner.

02

Agree the inputs

Start with relevant sales or sell-through, stock, open purchase orders, lead times and product definitions. Add returns, exchanges, promotions, costs or retailer data where the question needs them.

03

Investigate the signal

Check availability, promotions, seasonality, launches and channel changes. Use statistical or machine-learning methods where useful, with assumptions and limits visible.

04

Compare the options

Compare practical choices on quantity, timing or mix against supply constraints, service risk and cash exposure.

05

Review and recommend

Apply planning review and prepare a concise recommendation for your team to discuss and decide.

06

Maintain the work

Refresh the agreed analysis during the engagement and revisit the decisions when the evidence or constraints change.

Forecasts create evidence. Planning review gives it context.

A demand estimate is one input. We also examine what is available, what is already committed, what can arrive in time and what each option requires from cash and capacity.

Use the data that answers the question.

Demand

Sales and sell-through at the useful product, channel and time level. Separate opening orders from repeat demand where the data allows.

Stock and incoming supply

Available and committed stock, open purchase orders, arrival dates and relevant transfer constraints.

Operating constraints

Lead times, minimum order quantities, production slots, pack sizes and shelf life where relevant.

Commercial context

Promotions, launches, costs, returns or exchanges when they materially change the decision.

We agree the data sources and access method during scoping. The analysis reflects the coverage and quality of the available inputs.

A clear recommendation, with a clear owner.

ScaleSight maintains the agreed analysis, investigates exceptions, compares options and prepares the reviewed brief. Your team supplies operating context, validates constraints, chooses the action and executes through its own processes.

Can you work alongside our planning system?

Yes. We scope the additional analysis around your existing process and the decision that needs attention.

Is every engagement machine learning?

No. The question, data and decision horizon determine which methods are useful.

What if the data is incomplete?

We identify the gaps, state the assumptions and agree whether a narrower investigation can still be useful.

Will you place orders for us?

Your team retains approval and execution of buys, production and allocation.
