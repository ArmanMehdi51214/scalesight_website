Food / launch demand Illustrative · synthetic data

Is the launch adding demand or shifting the flavor mix?

Evidence to examine:

total portfolio sales, availability, opening fill, repeat orders and shared production capacity.

Options to compare:

increase total output, change the mix within the planned run or hold until repeat demand is clearer.

Decision output:

a next-run recommendation with component, capacity and cash implications.

Apparel / size depth Illustrative · synthetic data

Which size demand should shape the next buy?

Evidence to examine:

sales by size, in-stock exposure, returns, exchanges and retained demand.

Options to compare:

deepen selected sizes, rebalance the size curve or hold depth pending better evidence.

Decision output:

a size-depth recommendation with lead-time, stock and cash trade-offs.
