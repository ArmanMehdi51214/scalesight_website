Sequential Architecture

Four weeks, one agreed decision scope.

30-day decision cadence
01 Week 1

1 / Scope

Agree the decision, products, channels, owner, timing, inputs and constraints. Define how usefulness will be judged. Establish the first decision note and data-gap log.

02 Week 2

2 / Establish the read

Prepare and check inputs, distinguish demand from availability effects, and document the baseline and assumptions. Review the first evidence-led read.

03 Week 3

3 / Compare options

Investigate the change and compare feasible quantity, timing or mix choices against supply, service and cash constraints.

04 Week 4

4 / Review and choose

Review the recommendation, remaining uncertainty and learning. Agree whether to stop, refine the scope or continue with recurring support.
